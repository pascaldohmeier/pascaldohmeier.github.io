One lane. Ten pins. And a hall that keeps getting bigger.

In Strike Empire you bowl yourself on your home lane while visitors play on the other lanes — every pin that falls earns money. You upgrade lanes, pins and facilities, train your team and win leagues from the casual league all the way to the world championship. After that you open branches in ever bigger cities and, in the end, found your own federation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I bowl?

Put your finger down at the bottom of your lane — that's where the ball sits at the foul line. Then swipe up: wherever the line points is where the ball reaches the pins. A curve in your swipe adds hook: the ball skids through the oil and hooks at the back end, so it hits the pins at a sharper angle. Swiping fast gives more power. While swiping you see the whole path; if the target ring turns green, it's a strike candidate. Aim for the pocket between pins 1 and 3.

How do I earn money?

Every pin that falls pays, strikes and spares pay a multiple. Your own throws count for the whole hall, and several strikes in a row increase your money even more. On the other lanes visitors play on their own, even when the game is closed.

What can I upgrade?

Up to 24 lanes, lane care (surface, oil machine, ball return, scoreboard), pin sets in nine materials from maple to meteorite, four special pins and seven facilities: shoe rental, bar, cosmic bowling, pro shop, grandstand, training centre and youth academy. More reputation brings better visitors, shoe rental and the bar bring more of them.

How do the leagues work?

Eight leagues from the casual league to the world championship. On a match day you and your three regular players compete against four other teams; you bowl your ten frames yourself, and a live table shows the standings. For the title you need several match-day wins. A title brings a trophy and unlocks new pins, facilities and the next league. After each match day there is a two-minute break, and once a day a free daily tournament awaits.

How does my team get better?

Your regular players train on their own, even offline — quickly at first, slower and slower near the top. The training centre, the youth academy, bigger cities and the franchise handbook raise their limit.

What is a branch?

Once you've won the title in the district league, you can sell your hall and start over in the next, bigger city. In return you get brand value for the franchise handbook: seven tiers with three paths each, only one per tier. You can respec at any time, but it costs 30 %. The big leagues are only played in bigger cities. Trophies, special pins, sponsors, awards and cosmetics stay.

And after the world championship?

Found your own federation: everything starts over, but you get federation votes for permanent bonuses. On top of that, five variants with their own rules open up — nine-pin, candlepin, duckpin, 5-pin and "Only 3 lanes" — each with a reward that lasts forever.

What do sponsors do?

Twelve goals unlock twelve sponsors, for example a turkey or a converted split. Each gives its own bonus, and the number of slots is limited.

Ads?

Only when you start them yourself: via the "×3" ad button at the top left. A video gives you 30 minutes of triple money, even offline. Several videos add up to two hours. There are no ads that pop up in between and no banners. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I buy anything?

No. Strike Empire has no in-app purchases. Progress only comes from playing.

Do I need an account? Internet?

No. The game runs entirely on your iPhone, even in airplane mode. You only need internet for the optional ads.

Does the game keep running when I close it?

Yes. When you come back, the time is calculated: visitors pay for up to two hours at first, and your team trains the whole time. A skill and a sponsor extend that time.

What is the notification?

There is exactly one: "Your hall is closing up", when the offline time has run out. The game only asks whether it may send it after your first branch. You can turn it off in the iOS settings.

How do I back up my save or move it to a new iPhone?

More → Options → "Export" copies your save as text to the clipboard. Keep it somewhere, for example in a note, and copy it to the clipboard again on the new device. Then tap "Import". The save on that device is replaced.

Can I start over?

Yes: More → Options → "Delete everything". This deletes your entire save, including the handbook, federation, awards and statistics; your settings stay. It can't be undone — export your save first if you want to keep it.

Language and dark mode?

Strike Empire is available in German and English. It follows your iPhone's language, but you can also pick one in the options. The same goes for the appearance: System, Light or Dark.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
