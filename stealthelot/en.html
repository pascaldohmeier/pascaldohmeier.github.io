Swipe, crack, cash in

A parking lot full of cars. Swipe your finger across the rows, crack the locks and get cash from the fence. The better your tools, the classier the loot. No in-app purchases, ads only when you start them yourself.

How it works

  • Swiping: Every hit takes lock points off. Several cars in one swipe give combos up to ×3.
  • Tools: A grey padlock shows the tool level you need.
  • Shops and locations: Shops attract customers, later you move on to bigger parking lots.
  • Crew members crack whole rows, even when the game is closed.
  • Going underground: A restart with contacts that make everything stronger and buy the skill tree.
  • Paint shop and tuning open at 400 and 1,400 contacts in total.

Questions and answers

Will I lose my progress when I switch phones? Your save is stored only on the device. It comes along with an iPhone backup or when you move over with Quick Start.

The ad button loads and then nothing happens? If no ad arrives within 10 seconds, you get the reward anyway. Write to us if that's not the case.

How do I change my ad consent? Settings → "Change ad consent" (appears when Google requires it) and iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
