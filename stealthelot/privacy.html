Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · applies to Steal the Lot from version 1.0

Summary

Steal the Lot needs no account, no email address and no password. There is no server of ours and no analytics. Your save game stays on your iPhone only.

You only see ads when you start them yourself: a video for an in-game reward. There are no interstitials and no banners. For personalised ads we ask first — via Google's consent form and Apple's tracking prompt.

The app sends no notifications.

2 · Data that stays on your device

The app stores exactly this on your device — the complete list:

  • the save game as a file in the app's storage, with an automatic backup copy: your progress (money, tools, location, shops, contacts, skill tree, paint shop, gearbox, challenges, achievements), statistics (such as play time and cars cracked), when you last played (for offline earnings), until when an ad boost runs and which unlock scenes you have seen. The save game also holds your settings: language, appearance, number format, haptics and sound.

There is no identifier of ours — no user ID, no device ID — and nothing is linked to you. We do not transmit any of it. The app does not read the clipboard.

3 · Deleting data

  • In the app: Settings → “Reset game …”. Deletes all progress; your settings stay.
  • Delete the app: removes everything from the device.

4 · Ads (Google AdMob)

Provider for the European Economic Area: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Ads appear only when you start them: via the “×3” button (30 minutes of triple value in the game, several videos add up to two hours), via a short offer on the parking lot (cash case or customer rush) or in the offline report (double earnings). If no ad is available, you get the reward anyway. There are no ads between game sections and no banners. Google's ad SDK starts after 90 seconds of play at the earliest, or when you tap an ad button before that.

To deliver, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google processes, among other things:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you agreed to both the consent form and Apple's tracking prompt
  • a coarse location estimate from your IP address (country, region — no GPS; the app asks for no location permission)
  • device and usage signals and crash and performance data of the ad SDK
  • advertising data: which ads were shown and how you interacted with them

Google may use this data across apps for profiling. That is why we ask first. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); without it, Google only shows non-personalised ads.

You can withdraw your consent at any time: via “Change ad consent” in the game's settings. Apple's tracking permission is under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

More: Google's privacy policy, “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.

5 · Permissions

The only prompt is Apple's tracking dialog, which belongs to the ads (section 4). The app sends no notifications and asks for no access to location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

6 · If you write to us

If you email the address above, we process your email address and message to answer it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The mailbox provider is Google; messages are kept as long as needed for follow-up questions and then deleted.

7 · This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technically necessary data (IP address, time, page, user agent) in server logs. We set no cookies, load no third-party fonts or scripts and run no analytics.

8 · What we do not do

Steal the Lot has no server, no account, no in-app purchases, no Game Center, no notifications and no analytics or crash reporting. We collect no personal data ourselves, share none and sell none.

9 · Your rights

You have the rights under Art. 15–21 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for us: the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information). We cannot give information about your save game because we do not have it.

10 · Children

The game asks for no age, collects no data itself and offers no way to contact other people. It contains optional ads (section 4), as shown in the App Store age rating.

11 · Changes

We update this policy when the processing changes. The date above shows the latest version.
