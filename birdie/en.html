A driving range. A basket of balls. And a needle swinging back and forth.

In Birdie you practice on the range and earn money with every ball — the farther the target green and the closer to the flag, the more. Your clubs get better with every shot, a ball machine keeps hitting for you, and in tournaments you play against a field for prize money, trophies and the next course — from your first club tournament to the Grand Slam and beyond.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

At the bottom of the range a needle swings back and forth. Tap anywhere on the range while it is in the green zone — that's a perfect swing: the ball scatters less and brings more money and more experience. Otherwise you get a slice, a hook or a miss. Perfect shots in a row increase your money even further. With the money you buy better balls, bigger target greens, a swing trainer and equipment in the shop. Anything new is explained the first time it appears.

How do my clubs get better?

By practicing. Every ball gives experience to the club in your hand. With every level it scatters less, flies a bit farther, pulls off line less often, and its green zone gets wider. New clubs are in the pro shop, but only once you have reached the matching course — the driver, for example, only with the fourth.

What does the ball machine do?

It hits balls on its own, even when the game is closed. It never hits perfectly, and it gives only a little experience — you still have to practice yourself.

How do tournaments work?

There are nine courses. On the first four you play six holes, from the fifth on nine. Eleven players compete against you, wind and elevation count too, and every shot uses the needle, including the putt. A tournament has an entry fee; the top places pay back many times that. Your first win on a course gives you a trophy and opens the next course and new clubs. After each tournament there is a two-minute break. Once a day there is also a daily tournament with no entry fee.

What is a season?

Once you have won a tournament in a season, you can end it. Money and range upgrades then start over, and your clubs lose 30 % of their experience. In return you get training points for your best result on each course. Clubs, equipment, courses, trophies, sponsors and skills stay.

What is the skill tree?

Seven tiers with three paths each that you build up with training points — such as Long Hitter, Putt King or Night Shift. You can only choose one path per tier, so you have to decide. You can respec at any time, but it costs 30 % of the points.

What do sponsors do?

Twelve goals unlock twelve sponsors, for example 25 perfect shots in a row or a tournament under par. Each sponsor gives its own advantage. The number of sponsor slots is limited, so you choose.

What comes after the Grand Slam?

With a win on the ninth course you can end your career: everything starts over, and in return you get fame, which permanently brings more money, and perks in the Hall of Fame. It also opens the endless Legends Tour with courses that get harder tier by tier, and your own golf club: you build holes, a clubhouse and facilities, members pay fees, and in the Club Open you play on your own course.

Ads?

Only if you start them yourself: with the "×3 ad" button on the range. One video gives you 30 minutes of triple money, offline too. Several videos add up to two hours. There are no ads that pop up in between and no banners. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I buy anything?

No. Birdie has no in-app purchases. Progress comes only from playing.

Do I need an account? Internet?

No. The game runs entirely on your iPhone, even in airplane mode. You only need internet for the optional ads.

Does the game keep running when I close it?

Yes, as soon as you have a ball machine. When you come back, the time away is calculated: at first for up to two hours. Equipment, a sponsor and a skill extend that.

What is the notification?

There is exactly one: "The ball machine is empty", when the offline time has run out and the machine isn't earning anything anymore. The game only asks after your first season whether it may send it. You can turn it off in the iOS Settings.

How do I back up my save or move it to a new iPhone?

Settings → "Export" copies your save as text to the clipboard. Store it somewhere, for example in a note, and on the new device copy it to the clipboard again. Then Settings → "Import". The save on the device is replaced.

Can I start over?

Yes: Settings → "Delete everything". This deletes the entire save, including skills, Hall of Fame, golf club, achievements and statistics; your settings are kept. This cannot be undone — export your save first if you want to keep it.

Language and dark mode?

Birdie is available in German and English. It follows your iPhone's language, but you can also set it in the settings. The same goes for the appearance: System, Light or Dark.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
