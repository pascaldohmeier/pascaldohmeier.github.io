A tiny 3×3. A few digits. And every digit earns coins.

In Idoku you solve sudokus and get paid for it — the faster and more error-free, the more stars. With the coins you hire solvers who puzzle on their own: slow and sloppy at first, later fast and precise. They discover real sudoku techniques, and you unlock ever larger formats, up to 16×16.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more stars?

You get three stars when you solve a puzzle without mistakes and under the target time. One star is always guaranteed. Stars fill the bar to the next size.

What do the solvers do?

Each solver puzzles on their own tile. They only know the techniques they have already discovered; otherwise they guess and erase. With speed and care they get better, with experience they level up, and from level 10 they are a master.

What does "Hand in notebook" do?

From 9×9 and 1 trillion coins you can hand in your notebook. Coins, formats and upgrades start over, and in return you get insight for the knowledge tree. Each further notebook needs more coins. Later the publisher adds another layer.

What is the daily shift?

Once a day a fresh run with a special rule: 15 minutes, up to three daily stars. The stars only buy looks (paper, digit colors, frames …), no advantages.

Does the game keep running when the app is closed?

Yes. Your solvers keep working offline, at first at half earnings for up to 8 hours, more and longer with the knowledge tree.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play if you start them yourself — for example 30 minutes of triple earnings, double offline earnings or starting the daily shift. There are no ads in between, no banners and no purchases.

Where is my save?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app also deletes your save.
