One die. A big table. And a swipe of your finger.

In Pasch you swipe across your dice, and every pip earns euros. With them you buy more dice — made of wood, silver, gold, emerald, ruby, sapphire, amethyst, obsidian, diamond, platinum and later titanium and meteorite —, make them more valuable, let them roll by themselves and give them more sides, from the D6 to the D100. Equal numbers in one swipe are doubles, and they pay off.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Swipe your finger across the dice on the table — every die you hit rolls, and its pips turn into euros. With the money you buy new dice and upgrades: more value per pip, high numbers more often, auto-roll and more sides. Equal numbers in one swipe (doubles), straights or a full house give a bonus. On top of that there are golden dice to catch, the dice cup that throws all dice at once, and glowing fields you can aim for. New things are explained the first time.

What are the collapse and dark matter?

At some point growth gets sluggish. Then you can let the table collapse: money, dice and upgrades start over, and in return you get dark matter. It stays, and with it you buy nodes in the skill tree that make every further run faster. From the first collapse on there is also a D20 roll once a day.

What is the skill tree?

A tree of 79 nodes that you unlock with dark matter: more income, faster auto-roll, automatic buying, new materials, more offline time and much more. On the very outside lies a ring for the late game.

What are challenges?

Nine runs with tougher rules, for example only with wooden dice or without doubles. If you complete one, you get a permanent reward. You can quit at any time.

What are the Big Bang and stardust?

The second, bigger fresh start, possible after the last node in the outer ring. It also resets dark matter and the skill tree and gives you stardust. You spend it in the stardust shop — where you'll find titanium and meteorite dice, among other things.

Ads?

Only when you start them yourself: via the "×3 ad" button at the table. A video gives you 30 minutes of triple income, also offline. Several videos add up to two hours. There are no ads that interrupt you and no banners. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I buy anything?

No. Pasch has no in-app purchases. Progress comes only from playing.

Do I need an account? Internet?

No. The game runs entirely on your iPhone, even in airplane mode. You only need internet for the optional ads.

Does the game keep running when I close it?

Yes, as soon as your dice roll by themselves. When you come back, the elapsed time is calculated: at first at half income and for up to eight hours. Nodes in the skill tree raise this to 75 % and twelve hours, later to full income and 24 hours.

What is the notification?

There is exactly one: "Your auto-roll is taking a break", when the offline time is used up and your dice aren't earning anything anymore. The game only asks after your first collapse whether it may send it. You can turn it off in the iOS settings.

How do I back up my save or move it to a new iPhone?

Settings → "Export" copies your save as text to the clipboard. Store it somewhere, for example in a note, and copy it back to the clipboard on the new device. Then Settings → "Import". This replaces the save on the device.

Can I start over?

Yes: Settings → "Reset everything". This deletes your entire save, including skill tree, achievements, statistics and album; your settings stay. This cannot be undone — export your save first if you want to keep it.

Language and dark mode?

Pasch is available in German and English. It follows your iPhone's language, but you can also set it in the settings. The same goes for the appearance: System, Light or Dark.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
