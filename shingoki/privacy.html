Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026 · covers the “Shingoki” app

In short

Shingoki needs no account, no email address and no password. You only pick a display name for the leaderboards. We never learn who you are.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob and is funded by them. Ads can be removed permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

For personalised ads we ask you first — through Google's consent dialog and, on iOS, through Apple's tracking prompt as well. If you decline you still see ads, just not personalised ones. Everything else works exactly the same.

We run no tracking and no usage analytics. No Firebase, no Google Analytics, and no product events of our own either: the app does not record how you play.

2 · Data that never leaves your device

Your statistics, your streak, a paused game including the lines you have drawn and its undo history, your settings (language, appearance, sound, haptics, reminder, error display) and the list of players you have blocked. All of this is stored only in the app's local settings on your device. It is not transmitted and disappears when you delete the app.

Keeping the block list local is deliberate: blocking is a personal “I don't want to see this” and nobody's verdict — so it works offline and hides no one from other players.

4 · Advertising (Google AdMob)

Provider for the European Economic Area: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Ads appear in exactly two places: once when you leave the end screen of a solved daily puzzle, and as an optional video when you ask for a hint. Practice mode has no forced ads.

To deliver, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google processes among other things:

  • your device's advertising ID (IDFA) — only if you agreed to both the consent dialog and Apple's tracking prompt
  • a coarse location estimate from your IP address (country, region — no GPS data; the app requests no location permission)
  • device and usage signals as well as crash and performance data from the ad SDK
  • ad data: which ads you were shown and how you reacted

Google may also use this data across apps to build profiles. That is precisely why we ask first.

Your consent can be withdrawn at any time, without giving reasons, through the privacy options in Google's consent dialog. Apple's tracking permission is changed under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. A withdrawal takes effect from that moment; processing carried out before remains lawful.

Turning ads off entirely: the one-time in-app purchase “Remove ads”. After that the ad SDK is no longer loaded and no ad data is collected. You then get hints without a video.

More information: Google's privacy policy, “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services” and Google's data transfer frameworks.

10 · What we do not do

  • We collect no name, no email address, no phone number, no address book, no GPS location, no photos, no health and no payment data.
  • We run no usage analytics. There are no product events in this app, no analytics service and no record of how you play. Only the result of a solved puzzle reaches the server.
  • We do not sell data and pass it to no one beyond the recipients named in sections 3, 4, 5 and 8.
  • There is no automated decision-making and no profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Leaderboard placement follows solely from the time taken, and for the weekly and monthly boards from the number of days solved and the total time.
  • We operate no cross-app tracking of our own.

12 · Is providing data required?

You are not obliged to provide any data, and there is no statutory or contractual requirement to do so. The game itself works entirely without a server connection — puzzles are generated on your device, not downloaded. Anyone who prefers not to transmit anything simply does without the leaderboards. As for ads: without consent you see non-personalised ads; to have no ads at all there is the “Remove ads” purchase.

13 · Your rights

Delete your profile — right in the app, without asking us: Settings → “Profil löschen”. Display name, all leaderboard entries, your streak and your reports are removed from the server and the name becomes available again. You then continue as a new, anonymous player.

Beyond that you have the rights to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21), as well as the withdrawal of consent given (Art. 7(3)).

A practical note: we know you only by the random installation identifier. For an access request or a deletion we therefore need your display name — otherwise we cannot locate your profile, and we do not want to guess it from other traits.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority at any time (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent one is:

14 · Children

The app is not directed at children and is not classified as a kids app in the App Store categories. It does not ask for an age and collects no data that would indicate one. Display names are filtered automatically and can be reported by anyone; blocked players no longer appear on any leaderboard.

15 · Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the processing changes — for instance because a service is added or removed. The date at the top shows the current version. We announce material changes in the app.
