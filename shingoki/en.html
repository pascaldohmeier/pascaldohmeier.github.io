A grid of dots, a few circles with numbers. Find the one closed loop that fits them all.

Shingoki is a loop puzzle for iPhone. A new daily puzzle every day, the same for everyone worldwide, plus unlimited practice in three sizes. What counts is your time — no points, no levels.

The rules, complete

The board is a grid of dots. Edges run only horizontally and vertically between directly adjacent dots. You are looking for exactly one closed loop.

  • Every dot has 0 or exactly 2 loop edges — no branching, no crossing.
  • All drawn edges belong to one loop. Two separate loops are wrong.
  • The loop passes through every circle. Dots without a circle may stay empty.
  • White circle: the loop goes straight through it.
  • Black circle: the loop turns there.
  • The number is the sum of the lengths of the two straight arms, each up to the next turn, counted in edges. For a white circle that is the total length of the straight line it sits on; for a black one, arm A plus arm B. The smallest possible number is 2.
  • A circle can also appear without a number — then only the shape rule applies.

That is the complete list. Shingoki is not Masyu: there is no extra rule for the cells next to a circle.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. No email, no password. An anonymous profile is created automatically on first launch; you only choose a display name for the leaderboards.

Why can I only play the daily puzzle once?

Because everyone worldwide gets the same puzzle — one attempt per day keeps the comparison fair. The clock only starts with the first line you draw, so you can look at the puzzle at your leisure without it counting. If you want to keep playing, there is practice, which is unlimited.

I solved the daily puzzle and can't open it again.

You can — you'll see your recorded time instead of a new board. A second run would mean the best time could be rerolled as often as you like, and then the leaderboard would be worthless.

I lost my streak even though I only missed one day.

The app automatically repairs one missed day per calendar month. Two or more days in a row end the streak — otherwise the number wouldn't be honest.

Why does a hint cost a video?

Because the app is funded by ads and there would otherwise be no reason to show them at all. It is only fair because of a second decision: the clock stops while the ad is playing — a video never costs you the time you spent watching it. There are never more than three hints per puzzle.

The ad failed to load and I got the hint anyway.

That's how it should be. A broken ad request is our problem, not yours.

How do I get rid of the ads?

Settings → "Remove ads". A one-time purchase; after that, no ad SDK is loaded anymore and hints come without a video. The hint counter keeps running and the limit of three still applies — otherwise it would be a bought advantage. Use "Restore purchases" to bring the purchase to a new device.

How do I change my ad consent?

You can withdraw your consent to personalized ads at any time via the privacy options in the consent dialog. You change Apple's tracking permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → "Delete profile". Your display name, leaderboard entries and streak are removed from the server and the name becomes available again. You then continue playing as a new, anonymous player.

Someone is using an offensive name.

Press and hold the row in the leaderboard → "Report name" or "Block player". Blocked players no longer appear in any leaderboard.
