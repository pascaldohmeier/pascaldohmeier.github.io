A small pixel army. A fortress of blocks with numbers on them. And behind it the next one, a little tougher.

In Brickstorm your army fights on its own: warrior, archer, blade, mage and priest battle level by level through fortresses of numbered blocks until they fall. The crowns they earn along the way are yours to keep, and they make the next attempt stronger.

How the game works

A run

Every run starts at the first level. Your army attacks the fortress, and each block takes as many hits as the number on it. Once the fortress has fallen, you move on to the next level. At some point the army is too weak, and the run ends. That isn't a loss, it's how the game is meant to work: afterwards you start over, stronger.

Gold and crowns

You earn gold during a run and spend it there too, on units and upgrades that only last until the end of the run. You get crowns for every destroyed block, and they stay: you keep them across all runs.

Workshop

With crowns you buy permanent upgrades in the workshop: 53 of them in three areas, split into five tiers.

Lab

In the lab, research runs in real time. It takes minutes to hours and keeps going while the app is closed.

Cards and gems

You get gems for every boss, for every new record level, from the daily gift and optionally from ads. With them you open card packs, and the cards permanently strengthen your army.

Worlds

Reach level 30 in a world and the next one opens. The worlds go on endlessly, each tougher than the last and with more crowns.

Enemies

Later, new blocks appear: shield, healing, splinter, bomb and ice, each with its own effect. Even later, threats join in that change a whole run. Anything new is explained the first time it appears.

Frequently asked questions

My run is over. Is my progress gone?

No. Gold and the upgrades from the run expire, that's part of the game. Crowns, workshop, lab, cards and worlds stay with you.

Does the game keep running when I close it?

Research in the lab keeps running in real time and is done when you come back. Your army also collects crowns while you're away: a quarter of what you last earned per minute in the game, for at most eight hours (more with the workshop upgrade "Offline loot"). The game doesn't need internet for this and runs entirely on your iPhone, even in airplane mode. You only need internet for the optional ads.

Ads?

Only if you start them yourself. These are the places:

  • the "×3 ad" button: 30 minutes of triple crowns, several videos add up to two hours;
  • the speed button: the game runs faster than 1× for ten minutes after each video;
  • during a run, a small "Gold rain – watch ad" offer occasionally appears at the edge for a one-time gold bonus. You can dismiss it with ✕, it disappears on its own after a few seconds, and nothing plays unless you tap it;
  • "Keep fighting": if your army falls from level 5 on, you can keep fighting with a full army once per run after a video, or end the run;
  • "Double crowns" at the end of a run and "Double" for the crowns earned while you were away;
  • "Ad" in the Cards tab: +5 gems, at most five times a day.

If no ad is available at the moment, you get the reward anyway. There are no ads that pop up in between and no banners. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I buy anything?

No. Brickstorm has no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

When do I get the daily gift?

Once per calendar day, based on the date set on your iPhone.

Can I start over?

Yes: Options → "Start over". This deletes crowns, workshop, lab, cards, worlds and records. Sound and vibration are kept. This cannot be undone.

What happens if I delete the app?

Then your save is gone too. It only lives on your iPhone; there is no cloud copy and no way to export it.

Is the game available in English?

Yes. Since version 1.1, Brickstorm is available in nine languages: German, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. It follows your iPhone's language, and you can change it under Options → "Language".

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
