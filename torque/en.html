One gear. One crank. And lots and lots of orders of magnitude.

In Torque you build gearboxes — first at the workbench, then in the workshop, in your own factory, in the test center, in the group and finally on megaprojects. Every gear ratio puts load on the motor, every stage wears in, and behind every section a new fresh start awaits that makes you faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Tap the big crank at the top left. After 100 turns by hand you can install the first gear, and from then on the gearbox turns by itself. Everything else unlocks bit by bit and is explained the first time. You'll find the complete manual in the settings.

Do I need an account? Internet?

No. The game runs entirely on your iPhone, even in airplane mode. You only need internet for the optional ads and for the Game Center leaderboards.

Does the game keep running when I close it?

Yes. When you come back, the elapsed time is calculated. At first the workshop runs at half speed without you; from the first refounding on, the factory works the night shift and the time counts in full again.

Ads?

Only when you start them yourself. A video gives you, for example, four hours of double speed (stackable up to twelve hours, also runs offline). At most 20 per day, at least five minutes apart. There are no ads that interrupt you and no banners. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I buy anything?

No. Torque has no in-app purchases. Progress comes only from playing.

How do I back up my save or move it to a new iPhone?

Settings → "Export" copies your save as text to the clipboard. Store it somewhere, for example in a note, and copy it back to the clipboard on the new device. Then Settings → "Import".

Can I start over?

Yes: Settings → "Reset save". This deletes all your progress, achievements and statistics; your settings stay. This cannot be undone — export your save first if you want to keep it.

What are the notifications?

After your first retool, the game asks whether it may remind you: when retooling is worth it, when a boost has expired, and after a three-day break. Each of them can be turned off individually in the settings.

Leaderboards?

If you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, your best scores appear in Apple's leaderboards. Without Game Center, everything else works the same.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
