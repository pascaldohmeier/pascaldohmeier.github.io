Summary

Pathfinder Memory needs no account, no email address and no password — just a display name for the leaderboards. The app shows ads via Google AdMob and is funded by them; ads can be removed permanently with a one-time in-app purchase. For personalised ads we ask first, through Google's consent dialog and, on iOS, Apple's tracking prompt. Decline and you still see ads, just less relevant ones; everything else works unchanged.

1 · Data that never leaves your device

Progress, statistics, streaks, a paused run, your settings and your list of blocked players. Stored in the app's preferences and deleted with the app.

2 · Data on our server (Supabase, Frankfurt, Germany)

A random installation identifier (UUID) created on first launch; the display name you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboards); your results (date, levels cleared, streak); friendships (identifiers only); reports about display names; and product events (round started, level cleared or missed, tutorial finished) linked to that identifier, used only to see where the app is too hard or unclear.

Processor: Supabase, region eu-central-1. No third-party analytics — no Firebase, no Google Analytics.

3 · Advertising (Google AdMob)

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Dublin. For serving and measuring ads Google processes your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you agreed to both the consent dialog and Apple's tracking prompt — a coarse location estimate from your IP address, device and usage signals, crash and performance data from the ad SDK, and advertising data (which ads you were shown and how you reacted). Google may use this across apps to build a profile, which is why we ask first.

You can withdraw consent at any time via the privacy options in the consent dialog; Apple's tracking permission is changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The one-time in-app purchase “Remove ads” stops the ad SDK from loading at all.

4 · Not collected

Name, email, phone number, contacts, GPS location, photos, health data, payment details. Apple handles the in-app purchase; we receive no personal data from it.

5 · Legal bases

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for leaderboards, friends and profile; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for moderation and product events; Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) plus § 25(1) TDDDG for personalised ads and access to the advertising identifier; Art. 6(1)(f) for non-personalised ads.

6 · Retention

Leaderboard and profile data until you delete your profile; product events for at most 12 months. Google applies its own retention periods to advertising data.

7 · Your rights

Delete your profile any time in the app: Settings → “Delete profile”. This removes your display name, all leaderboard entries, streaks and friendships from the server and releases the name. You also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8 · Children

The app is not directed at children. Display names are filtered automatically and can be reported by anyone; blocked players no longer appear on any leaderboard.
