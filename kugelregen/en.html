One ball. A triangle of pegs. And slots at the bottom that pay the most at the edges.

In Kugelregen you swipe across the balls in the chute, and they fall from peg to peg into the slots. Every slot pays euros; the outer ones are rare and pay huge. With that you buy more balls — made of clay, glass, steel, copper, silver, gold, jade, ruby, diamond and later plasma, neutrons and black holes —, make them more valuable and let them drop on their own.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Swipe your finger across the balls in the chute at the top. Every ball you hit rolls a little in the direction of your swipe and then falls through the board of pegs. Where it lands is random — the slots at the edges are rare but pay many times more. All the balls from one swipe form a volley: if several land in the same slot, you get a bonus. With the money you buy new balls and upgrades: more value, spin (the ball keeps its direction and lands at the edge more often), a dropper machine and more rows. Anything new is explained the first time it appears.

What is melting down and what are sparks?

Once you have 10 billion euros in one run, you can melt down: money, balls and upgrades start over, and in return you get sparks. Every spark you have ever earned makes everything more valuable, and you spend them on nodes in the workshop — a skill tree with 82 nodes, with a ring on the very outside for the late game. From your first meltdown on there is also a daily drop once a day.

What are challenges?

Nine runs with harder rules, such as clay balls only or a tilted board. Complete one and you get a permanent reward. You can quit at any time.

What is the comet?

The second, bigger restart, available after the last node in the ring. It resets sparks and the workshop and gives you comets. They make everything permanently more valuable, and in the observatory you spend them on new ball types and helpers.

What is the arcade?

The third layer, from two comets on. A new arcade resets comets and everything below them and gives you tokens. With them you buy six machines, each with its own board rule: Mirror, Storm, Twin, Cascade and Gold Rush alongside the classic board. Once you own them all, you have finished the game — but the balls keep falling anyway.

Ads?

Only if you start them yourself: with the "×3 ad" button at the top of the board. One video gives you 30 minutes of triple earnings, offline too. Several videos add up to two hours. There are no ads that pop up in between and no banners. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I buy anything?

No. Kugelregen has no in-app purchases. Progress comes only from playing.

Do I need an account? Internet?

No. The game runs entirely on your iPhone, even in airplane mode. You only need internet for the optional ads.

Does the game keep running when I close it?

Yes, as soon as you have a machine that drops balls on its own. When you come back, the time away is calculated: at first at half earnings and for up to eight hours. Workshop nodes raise that to 75 % and twelve hours, later to full earnings and 24 hours.

Can I start over?

Yes: Options → "Delete save". This deletes the entire save, including the workshop, achievements, statistics and album. Language, number format, colors, sounds and vibration are kept. This cannot be undone.

Language and dark mode?

Kugelregen is available in German and English. It follows your iPhone's language, but you can also set it in the options. The same goes for the colors: System, Light or Dark.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
