A grid. Hidden mirrors. A ball. Where does it come out?

Pathfinder Memory is a daily memory puzzle for iPhone: memorize where the mirrors are — then they disappear, and you predict which gate the ball leaves the grid through. A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone worldwide.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. No email, no password. An anonymous profile is created automatically on first launch; you only choose a display name for the leaderboards.

Why can I only play the daily puzzle once?

Because everyone worldwide gets the same puzzle — one attempt per day keeps the comparison fair. If you want to keep playing, there is the practice mode, which is unlimited.

I lost my streak even though I only missed one day.

The app automatically repairs one missed day per month. Two or more days in a row end the streak — otherwise the number wouldn't be honest.

How do I get rid of the ads?

Settings → "Remove ads". A one-time purchase; after that, no ad SDK is loaded anymore. Use "Restore purchases" to bring the purchase to a new device.

How do I change my ad consent?

You can withdraw your consent to personalized ads at any time via the privacy options in the consent dialog. You change Apple's tracking permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → "Delete profile". Your display name, leaderboard entries, streaks and friendships are removed from the server and the name becomes available again. You then continue playing as a new, anonymous player.

Someone is using an offensive name.

Press and hold the row in the leaderboard → "Report name" or "Block player". Blocked players no longer appear in any leaderboard.
